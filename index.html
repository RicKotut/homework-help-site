How it works

Three steps, usually under ten minutes from question to understanding.

  1. 1

    Submit your question

    Type it out or snap a photo of the problem. Add whatever you've already tried — it helps your helper meet you where you are.

  2. 2

    Get matched with a helper

    Someone who knows the subject reads your question and starts a live chat with you, usually within a few minutes.

  3. 3

    Work through it, don't just copy it

    Your helper walks the reasoning with you step by step, so the next similar problem is one you can solve on your own.
