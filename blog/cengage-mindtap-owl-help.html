Online Course Platforms

Cengage MindTap and OWL Help

Part of the Stuck on an Online Course Platform guide

Cengage homework help requests tend to come from two kinds of assignments. One is a long multi-part problem where one early mistake ruins every later answer. The other is a platform that marks a right answer wrong because of rounding or units. Both have fixes that don’t depend on the platform.

How MindTap and OWL are set up

Cengage MindTap usually presents the course as a learning path: readings, videos, activities, and quizzes in the order your instructor chose. OWLv2 homework is Cengage’s online homework system for chemistry and other sciences, with randomized values and, depending on how your instructor set it up, hints and tutorials. Cengage accounting homework and other Cengage assignment types work in a similar way, with problems that generate new numbers for each student.

Where students lose points

  • Rounding too early. In a multi-part problem, rounding an early result changes every later answer.
  • Significant figures and units in chemistry, where a correct value in the wrong form is marked wrong.
  • Attempt limits. Each wrong submission can count against you, so guessing is expensive.
  • Skipping the learning path. MindTap activities often feed the assignments that follow.

A routine for multi-part problems

  1. Write the whole solution on paper first, before entering anything.
  2. Keep full calculator precision through every step and round only at the end, in the form the question asks for.
  3. Check units and significant figures against the information the question gives.
  4. Enter the answers for each part only after you’ve checked each one against the previous one.

Example: units and significant figures

A chemistry problem gives 0.5 mol of solute in 0.25 L of solution. Molarity = 0.5 ÷ 0.25 = 2.0 M.

If the problem gives its data to two significant figures, enter 2.0 M. Entering 2 M, or 2.00 M, can be marked wrong even though the number is the same.

Looking for Cengage homework answers?

Searches like Cengage homework answers, Cengage MindTap answers, Cengage assignment solutions, or an OWL Cengage chemistry answer key come from the same frustration: a problem that won’t accept your work. Randomized values mean a solution for another student’s numbers won’t match yours. Cengage accounting homework answers have the same problem, and a copied entry teaches nothing about why the debits and credits go where they do. The tutorials and a clear method do more for you than a key that doesn’t fit your numbers.

Thinking about paying someone to take your course?

If you’re considering paying someone to take your Cengage course, read Can You Pay Someone to Take Your Online Class? first. It covers the risks, and what to do if you’re too far behind to catch up alone.

Common mistakes

Rounding before the final step. Carry full precision through, and round once at the end.

Entering the right value in the wrong form. Always check the units and significant figures the question asks for.
