Online Course Platforms

McGraw Hill Connect Help

Part of the Stuck on an Online Course Platform guide

McGraw Hill Connect homework help requests usually involve one of two things: an accounting problem that’s fully right except for how it’s entered, or a reading assignment that keeps cycling back because the software doesn’t think you know the material yet. Both have routines that work.

How Connect is set up

Connect is McGraw Hill’s online platform for assignments, quizzes, and exams tied to your textbook. Many courses also use SmartBook, an adaptive reading tool that asks questions as you read and has you rate your confidence. Connect accounting problems are typically multi-step, with journal entries, ledgers, and statements. Connect biology courses often lean on SmartBook and question sets. As with other platforms, your instructor controls the attempts, deadlines, and which help tools you see.

Connect accounting: entering entries correctly

Journal entry problems tend to be graded on the accounts, the amounts, and the order, so a correct idea entered the wrong way can lose credit. Use this routine:

  1. List the accounts affected before you enter anything.
  2. Decide debit or credit for each using the accounting equation: assets = liabilities + equity.
  3. Enter debits first, then credits, in the order and format Connect expects.
  4. Confirm total debits equal total credits before you submit.

Example: a journal entry

A company buys $500 of supplies on credit.

Debit Supplies 500 (an asset increases). Credit Accounts payable 500 (a liability increases).

Debits (500) equal credits (500), and the debit is listed before the credit.

Connect biology and SmartBook

SmartBook works best when you answer honestly. Marking a guess as “I know it” makes the software treat the topic as learned, and it shows up again later when you can’t answer it. Be honest about confidence, and use the topics it keeps returning to as your review list.

Looking for McGraw Hill Connect answers?

Students search for McGraw Hill Connect answers, McGraw Hill test answers, McGraw Hill Connect accounting answers, or Connect biology answers when they’re running out of time. Connect randomizes values, so an answer for one version won’t fit yours, and SmartBook builds each student’s path from their own responses. A copied entry also won’t help on the exam, where you’ll have to build the entry from scratch.

Common mistakes

Submitting an entry without checking that debits equal credits. It’s the quickest way to catch an error before it costs an attempt.

Rating guesses as “I know it” in SmartBook. It reduces the review you actually need.
