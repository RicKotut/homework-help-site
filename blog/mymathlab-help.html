Online Course Platforms

MyMathLab Help: How to Get Unstuck

Part of the Stuck on an Online Course Platform guide

MyMathLab, which Pearson now calls MyLab Math, grades your work instantly and explains very little when you’re wrong. That’s why so many students search for MyMathLab homework help: they understand the lecture, then lose points to the way the software works.

How MyMathLab is set up

Pearson MyMathLab homework, quizzes, and tests usually generate problems with randomized numbers, so you and a classmate can see the same problem type with different values. In most MyMathLab classes your instructor controls how many attempts you get, the deadlines, any time limits, and which help tools appear. That’s why two students can describe MyMathLab very differently.

The built-in tools most students overlook

  • View an Example — walks through a similar problem with different numbers.
  • Help Me Solve This — guides you through the problem you’re actually stuck on, usually asking for your input at each step, then gives you a similar problem to do for credit.
  • Similar Question — generates a new version of the problem so you can practice the same method.
  • The eText and video links — jump to the exact section covering the topic.

These are often turned off on tests, so don’t build your whole approach around them. Learn the method well enough to work without them.

A routine that works when you’re stuck

  1. Try the problem on paper first, before touching any help tool.
  2. If you can’t start, open View an Example and follow the method on the similar problem.
  3. If you’re stuck partway, use Help Me Solve This to see the step you’re missing.
  4. Close the help and do a similar question on your own. If you can solve it without help, you’ve learned the method.
  5. Write the method in your own words, in one or two lines, for review before the quiz.

Why a correct answer gets marked wrong

A problem says to round to two decimal places. Your calculator shows 3.14159. Entering 3.14159 or 3.1 may both be marked wrong; only 3.14 is accepted.

The same thing happens with form. If the instructions ask for a fraction, entering 0.75 can be rejected where 3/4 is expected. Read the answer instructions under every problem, because the software checks the form of your answer as well as its value.

Looking for MyMathLab answer keys?

Because the problems are randomized, a MyMathLab answer key for someone else’s version rarely matches yours. You’d be checking against different numbers. Even if you found a match, it would cover the homework and not the tests, and the tests are usually where the grade is decided, often with the help tools off and sometimes proctored. A posted answer key can’t teach you the method, and the method is what the test checks.

What works better is the routine above, plus someone who can explain the step that isn’t clicking.

Thinking about paying someone to take your MyMathLab course?

Many students who search for “take MyMathLab course” are far behind and out of options, but paying someone carries real risks, from academic misconduct findings to scams. We cover them honestly in Can You Pay Someone to Take Your Online Class?, along with better ways to recover.

Common mistakes

Guessing on a limited-attempt problem. If you only get two or three attempts, a guess uses one up without teaching you anything. Work it out on paper first.

Ignoring the formatting instructions. Rounding, fraction versus decimal, and units account for a lot of correct-but-marked-wrong answers.
